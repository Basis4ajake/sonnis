**Sonnis turns a Nintendo 2DS or 3DS into a Sonos remote.** It talks to your speakers directly over your home Wi-Fi, with no Sonos account, no cloud and no phone. The top screen shows what's playing; the touch screen has the controls.

This is the first public release. It's a hobby project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Sonos, Inc.

## Highlights

- **Finds your speakers on its own** and remembers the last room you used. You can also enter a speaker's IP by hand.
- **Full playback control:** play/pause, next/previous, shuffle, repeat, mute, and volume for one room or the whole group (L/R or the touch slider).
- **Now Playing:** title, artist, album, album art, radio stream titles and progress.
- **Browse your system:** Sonos Favorites, Sonos Playlists, the Queue, your music library (artists, albums, tracks, genres), radio, and recently played.
- **Rooms and groups:** see every room, switch rooms, join and leave groups, and pin your favorite rooms to the top.
- **Make it yours:** 8 themes (including an animated tie-dye one), 8 accent colors, custom colors (by editing `settings.ini`), 12/24-hour clock, and optional **vinyl mode**, where the album art spins as a record at a true 33⅓ rpm.
- **Song scenes:** animated scenes across both screens for certain artists. Play the Grateful Dead, The Disco Biscuits, or Ozzy/Black Sabbath to see them.
- **Prince of Darkness vocabulary (⚠️ explicit):** an optional Ozzy-flavored wording mode with strong language throughout, room names included. It's **off by default**: START → Vocabulary.
- …and a few surprises for you to find.

## Which file do I download?

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `sonnis-1.0.0.zip` | **Most people.** Extract it to the root of your SD card, then launch Sonnis from the Homebrew Launcher. |
| `sonnis.cia` | A **Home Menu icon**. Install it with [FBI](https://github.com/lifehackerhansol/FBI). Same app; settings are shared with the `.3dsx`. |
| `sonnis.3dsx` | The bare app file, if you'd rather copy it to `sdmc:/3ds/sonnis/` yourself. |

## Before you start

- Your console needs to **run homebrew** (Luma3DS + Homebrew Launcher). If it doesn't yet, see [3ds.hacks.guide](https://3ds.hacks.guide).
- The 2DS/3DS and your speakers must be on the **same home network**. The console only does **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi** (WPA2), but your speakers can be on 5 GHz as long as your router bridges both bands (most do).
- **Tested on a Nintendo 2DS.** It should work on any 3DS-family console. Reports from other models are very welcome.

## Known limits

- **No Spotify / Apple Music / Amazon browsing.** Sonos only offers those through its cloud API, which needs modern encryption the 3DS can't do. **Workaround:** add albums, playlists or stations to *Sonos Favorites* in the official app; Sonnis plays Favorites directly, streaming-service content included.
- **"Recently played"** only covers what you started from your 2DS/3DS.
- **Album art from https-only sources** shows a placeholder for the same reason.

## Found a bug or have an idea?

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Basis4ajake/sonnis/issues). For connection problems, **START → Network diagnostics** and **START → Network log** show what's going on, and the README has a troubleshooting section.
